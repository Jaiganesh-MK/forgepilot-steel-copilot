# Permeability recovery playbook

Severe permeability loss is indicated by high pressure_drop_kpa with low permeability_index. It may be accompanied by low gas utilization, unstable burden descent, hanging/slip risk, poor gas distribution, or edge-heavy burden condition. During permeability loss, avoid aggressive wind increase, aggressive PCI increase, or production push.

# Operator levers for gas-flow restriction

A permeability recovery response may include reducing wind volume, temporarily reducing PCI, increasing coke support, using a permeability-recovery burden distribution pattern such as center-coke support or reduced edge loading, and checking tapping/hearth conditions. Top pressure changes should be conservative; a small change may damp instability only if plant constraints allow and restriction is not worsened.

# Evidence requirements

Use the pressure drop, permeability index, gas utilization, burden distribution mode, stockline asymmetry, and recent slip/hang indicators as evidence. If pressure_drop_kpa is above about 180 and permeability_index is below about 55, treat the case as severe and prioritize recovery over productivity.
