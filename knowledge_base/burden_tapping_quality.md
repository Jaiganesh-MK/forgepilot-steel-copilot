# Burden distribution, tapping, and hot-metal quality

Burden distribution affects gas-flow balance, permeability, thermal profile, and descent stability. Tapping and hearth state influence pressure behavior and operational risk. Hot-metal quality, especially silicon, is a useful thermal proxy.

# Burden distribution playbook

If the burden pattern is edge-heavy, stockline asymmetry is elevated, permeability is low, or pressure drop is high, recommend a plant-approved permeability-recovery burden pattern such as center-coke support or reduced edge loading. Do not recommend burden changes without referencing plant-approved matrices.

# Tapping and hearth playbook

If hearth liquid level is elevated and permeability/pressure-drop risk is high, recommend expedited tapping or cast-house priority review. If hearth level is normal, use normal tapping priority and monitoring.

# Quality playbook

Low silicon with low hot metal temperature and negative thermal index supports a cold thermal diagnosis. High silicon with high temperature supports hot-state diagnosis. Quality actions should coordinate with thermal and fuel agents rather than isolated quality-only setpoint moves.
