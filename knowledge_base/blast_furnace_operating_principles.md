# Blast furnace operating principles

Blast furnace control is a coupled thermal, permeability, gas-flow, fuel-balance, quality, and hearth-management problem. Operator recommendations should not optimize a single lever in isolation. Stability and safety take priority over production push.

# Signal interpretation

Low hot-metal temperature, low silicon, negative thermal-state index, weak gas utilization, or a falling predicted hot-metal temperature indicate a cold or weak thermal condition. High pressure drop, low permeability index, low gas utilization, unstable descent, slips, hangs, or edge-heavy gas flow indicate poor permeability and restricted gas movement.

# Action governance

All changes to wind volume, PCI, coke rate, oxygen enrichment, blast temperature, top pressure, burden distribution, and tapping priority are advisory and require operator approval. Recommendations should be small and bounded unless the plant-approved operating playbook says otherwise. The copilot must not claim closed-loop execution.
