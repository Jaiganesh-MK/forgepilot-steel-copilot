# PCI, coke, and total fuel balance

PCI improves fuel efficiency but can worsen thermal reserve and permeability if pushed during unstable operation. Coke provides both heat and structural support. During poor permeability or cold operation, coke support and PCI reduction may be safer than chasing minimum fuel rate.

# PCI advisory logic

Reduce or hold PCI when permeability index is low, pressure drop is high, gas utilization is weak, or thermal state is cold. Increase PCI only when thermal reserve, gas flow, and permeability are stable. PCI changes should be coordinated with coke rate, oxygen enrichment, and blast temperature.

# Coke-rate advisory logic

Increase coke support when the furnace is cold or permeability is poor. Avoid large coke changes when the signals are weak or conflicting. If operation is stable and total fuel rate is high, recommend optimization only after confirming stable thermal and gas-flow conditions.
