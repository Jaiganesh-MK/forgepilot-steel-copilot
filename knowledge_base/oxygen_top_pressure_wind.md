# Wind volume, oxygen enrichment, and top pressure

Wind volume, oxygen enrichment, and top pressure are high-impact gas-flow and productivity levers. They must be coordinated with permeability and thermal state.

# Wind-volume logic

Increase wind only when permeability is stable, pressure drop is not elevated, and thermal reserve is adequate. Hold or reduce wind when pressure drop is high or permeability index is low. During severe restriction, a small wind reduction can support recovery.

# Oxygen enrichment logic

Oxygen enrichment can support thermal intensity but should be limited during severe permeability restriction. In a cold furnace with manageable gas-flow risk, a small oxygen increase may be useful. In a cold furnace with severe permeability loss, use only a modest increase if raceway stability and plant constraints allow.

# Top-pressure logic

Top pressure can support gas utilization, but aggressive top-pressure increases during restriction can worsen instability. When gas utilization is low and permeability is manageable, small top-pressure increases may be considered. During severe restriction, hold or use only small adjustments with operator confirmation.
